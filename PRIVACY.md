# Privacy

> **TL;DR:** Product OS collects nothing. It is a set of instructions, templates and one board page that run inside your own Claude account. Its author receives no data from you.

## What Product OS Is

Product OS is a plugin for Claude, written by John De La Torre-Ugarte and published as open source. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do with it is sent to its author.

## What Is Read When You Use It

1. What you type, attach and link in your Claude chat.
2. What Claude reads through the connectors you have connected yourself, such as your Jira, Confluence and Figma. This can include names and email addresses that are already in those tools.

## Where It Is Kept

1. **Your Claude account:** your chats, the documents and designs Claude makes, and the project board with its task and document list.
2. **Your Atlassian site:** the Confluence pages and Jira tickets you approved, after your yes to a publish plan.
3. **Your Figma account:** the designs you asked Claude to make or change there.

Product OS keeps no copy anywhere else. The board page loads nothing from outside Claude, and only people you share the board with can see it.

## The Services You Use With It

Your use of Claude, Atlassian and Figma is covered by the terms and privacy policies of Anthropic, Atlassian and Figma. Product OS does not change them.

## Removing Your Data

Delete the chats, documents, boards, pages and tickets in the account where each one lives. Removing the plugin from Claude removes Product OS itself.
