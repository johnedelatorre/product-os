      <p>A one-minute guide. Open it any time with the Guide button at the top.</p>

    </header>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li><b>Claude leads, in your chat</b>It asks one question at a time. Tap an answer, or type your own.</li>
      <li><b>An agent takes each step</b>Claude brings in the right one, such as the PRD Author, and says who is working.</li>
      <li><b>You decide</b>Nothing is written before your yes to the plan. Nothing is published before your yes to the publish plan.</li>
      <li><b>This board keeps score</b>It updates by itself. Every answer goes in the chat. The one thing you do here is tick a task when it is done.</li>
    </ol>

        <ul class="says ask">
          <li><b>When do I start a new chat?</b><span>You do not have to decide. Before a large phase, such as hundreds of design screens, Claude tells you the size in your chat and asks one question: a second chat, yes or no. A small feature stays in one chat.</span></li>
          <li><b>Who does it?</b><span>You start the new chat, and it takes one paste. Claude does the rest. It writes the handover onto this board first, so the new chat knows the whole job.</span></li>
          <li><b>Where are my instructions?</b><span>In two places. Claude gives you the steps in the chat when you say yes. And this board has the prompt: open the row in the Workstreams card and select <q>Copy the prompt</q>.</span></li>

          <li><b>How do I see how it is going?</b><span>The Workstreams card shows each part of the project: its step, its status and when it was last updated. Or ask in any chat for the status of a workstream, by the name you gave it.</span></li>
